Guided game resolution

A tested, limited automation system based on the English comprehensive rules v2.10. This is not a claim of full rules compliance or support for every card. Unknown abilities, replacement effects, and newer Japanese trigger types require manual resolution.

Automatic flow

Routine timing checks, battle comparison, damage reveals, empty counter windows and normal turn cleanup now continue without acknowledgment prompts. Trigger and damage cards briefly appear on screen while still in the resolution zone, then move to their correct destination. Both players’ piles open directly when clicked; individual cards inside have inspection and context actions. Pause stops automatic progression for manual effects or review.

Genuine choices still pause: eligible counter cards, trigger targets, choosing a level card, meaningful multi-icon order, discard choices and payable Encore. Optional Draw/Pool/Treasure top-card effects are taken automatically by default; Effects & corrections lets you ask each time instead. The six non-level clock cards keep their existing order without six extra prompts. Undo and pile tools can correct an ordering decision.

Attacks and core procedures

Check timings run automatically in the required order. Recognized trigger timings open a prompt with the printed effect. If its instructions are unsupported, the prompt asks the player to check the conditions and resolve them manually. Other unsupported text appears in the review panel. This does not grant a free event or ACT timing. Direct attacks are chosen when no opponent faces the attacker; otherwise frontal or side is selected. The attacker rests. Attack-type adjustments and Soul trigger gains last until end of turn; a side adjustment is fixed using the defending level at declaration. Supported continuous effects recalculate from the current stage and climax area.

Trigger cards are revealed in resolution; choose icon order for multiple icons. The trigger card enters stock after icon effects unless its effect already moved it. Only frontal attacks have a counter play timing. At most one counter is accepted by the guide. Simple supported Backup is paid and applied; eligible counter events require manual verification of every printed requirement and manual payment/effect handling. Battle automatically compares calculated power, including recorded modifiers, and reverses the weaker character, both on a tie.

Damage cards remain in resolution until cancellation or the complete instance is resolved. Cancellation moves that instance's revealed cards to waiting. Uncanceled damage goes to clock in reveal order. Shot uses a new damage instance. Refresh is an immediate interrupt: only waiting-room cards form the shuffled deck, followed immediately by one top card to clock; this is not cancelable damage. Level-up interrupts, choosing one of the bottom seven clock cards for level and moving the other six in their existing order to waiting. Excess clock remains. The owner chooses the order of simultaneous refresh and level-up; turn-player procedures precede non-turn-player procedures. Multi-part cost suspension is available in Effects & corrections.

Encore processes reversed characters one at a time, turn player first. Standard top-three-stock Encore is supported. Other Encore abilities remain manual. End phase guides discard to seven, climax cleanup, turn-duration expiration and check timings, then stand and draw for the next turn. The optional clock action and queued draws resume after rule interrupts.

Exactly which card abilities are recognized

Support is tied to the complete recognized ability text, not a blanket promise for a card or series. The imported records contain 9,296 recognized ability lines, including language equivalents and artwork variants. Other lines on those same cards remain manual.

  • Unconditional continuous “all your characters” +power/+soul, or +soul alone. Common +1000 power/+1 soul and +2 soul climaxes are included; these apply only while the source remains in its valid area.
  • Continuous self +power or +soul, unconditional, during your turn, or during your opponent's turn.
  • Continuous self +soul in the middle center-stage position.
  • Unconditional Assist: fixed +power to characters in front of a back-stage source.
  • Unconditional on-attack self +power or +soul until end of turn.
  • Supported hand-to-stage or hand-to-climax placement AUTO: optional draw clause followed by choosing one character for fixed +power/+soul, or a fixed +soul placement effect. The printed effect is mandatory where its text says so; you choose the target.
  • Simple Backup with fixed power, printed level and numeric stock cost plus discarding the Backup card from hand. Extra AUTO effects on the Backup card remain manual.

Download the exact recognized card codes and ability text (CSV). Japanese records use a compatible official English equivalent where available; no translation is invented. Trigger reminder text does not count as a played climax ability.

Manual effects and limitations

  • All other conditions, trait/name restrictions, climax combos, attack restrictions, replacements, granted abilities, modified trigger counts, damage prevention/redirects, and complex costs remain manual. Other-zone abilities, especially memory, markers, clock and hidden-zone abilities, require player review.
  • Record soul, power or level adjustments with Effects & corrections. Turn modifiers expire at end cleanup. Persistent modifiers must be removed when their printed condition or duration ends. Conditions and durations outside the recognized families do not recalculate automatically.
  • The nonblocking review panel shows unrecognized stage/climax/top-clock text. Resolve mandatory pending AUTO abilities; do not simply skip them. Players determine eligibility and order of unsupported abilities and resolve them using manual tools. Pause and manual correction tools remain available; additional damage must wait until the current damage instance is finished.
  • Record manual power modifiers before battle; the comparison itself no longer asks for confirmation. Special reversal prevention, insufficient-power removal, illegal-zone cleanup and replacement/trigger-count effects are not generally automated.
  • The ability system does not implement a universal rules engine for every interaction. Manual corrections during a pending sequence can invalidate its stored targets; abort or undo and redeclare if required. Stock remains ordered; ordinary stock payments use the top cards. Nonstandard stock selection is manual.
  • Undo/redo saves complete in-memory game snapshots, including card order, queued steps, pending choices, recorded modifiers and damage instances. The active match and pending choices survive reload; undo history itself lasts for the current session.

Card ability prompts

Supported patterns include certain on-play, attack, reversal, stage-to-waiting-room and Backup effects. Optional effects can be declined. Required effects have a Resolve button. The game can select targets, look at and move top cards, pay supported Stock and discard costs, search and shuffle the deck, and enforce the printed once-per-turn limit for supported ACT abilities. Conditional and compound card text beyond these patterns still requires manual resolution.

Verification

Automated tests cover all attack types; soul and supported climax effects; optional trigger choices, multi-icon ordering and Standby replacement; Backup and stock order; canceled/uncanceled damage and independent Shot; excess clock and level ordering; refresh and level-up interrupting damage; refresh during trigger and draw; simultaneous procedures and cost deferral; level-four/empty-deck loss; climax entry effects; Encore/end/stand/draw; full-state undo/redo and duplicate input rejection. The saved-deck, Japanese-preview and manual-mode regression suites remain in place.

Rules: official rules index and English comprehensive rules v2.10, especially 4.10, 4.12, 6–9 and 10.2. Newer Japanese-only mechanics have not been verified against a newer Japanese comprehensive-rules edition.
